# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two distinct audiences on one site:
1. **Corporate travel buyers / event planners (primary, B2B)** — booking on behalf of a company for MICE, incentive travel, or corporate ticketing. They're evaluating credibility and track record before ever picking up the phone; this is a considered, relationship-driven purchase, not an impulse click.
2. **Leisure / FIT travelers (secondary, B2C)** — individuals or families wanting a customized trip, visa help, or forex, not a self-serve booking engine. They want to feel this is a real, established operator worth trusting with a passport and a payment, not a template site running Lorem Ipsum (see `company_info.md` §5 — that's literally the problem with the *current live* absolutetravels.in).

Neither audience searches/filters/books live inventory here. The job is: build enough trust and clarity in one visit that they submit an inquiry or call. This is a request-a-quote / talk-to-a-person business, not an OTA marketplace — that shapes what "modern" can mean here (see Anti-references).

## Product Purpose

Convert visits into inquiries (calls, WhatsApp, form submissions) by presenting Absolute Event & Travel Services as an established (11+ years), full-service operator — corporate MICE, leisure, and travel documentation (visa/passport/forex) under one roof — replacing a badly outdated 2016-template site that undermines that claim on sight.

## Brand Personality

**Established / trustworthy** (user-confirmed). Not stiff-corporate, not startup-cute. Reads as: "we have done this a thousand times, hand it to us." Confidence earned through specificity (real numbers, real process, real people) rather than generic reassurance copy.

Redesign brief: evolve the current gold/navy/porcelain identity rather than replace it — push it toward more visual conviction (bolder CTAs, more confident use of the accent colors, a real trust-signal color language) rather than toward a different brand altogether. See Design Principles.

## Anti-references

- **Literal OTA/booking-platform look** (Expedia/TripAdvisor blue-green-orange, search-bar-as-hero, listing-grid UX). Explicitly discussed and rejected as a structural/color direction — this business has no live inventory to search, and the brand equity in gold/navy shouldn't be diluted toward a marketplace aesthetic that doesn't fit the buying model. What *is* being borrowed from those sites: their trust/social-proof language (reviews, verified badges, credibility-through-numbers) — reinterpreted through emerald as a "verified/trusted" signal color, not their palette.
- **Generic corporate-blue SaaS.** Already named in `DESIGN.md`'s "memorable thing" — the site should not read as interchangeable enterprise software.
- **The current live absolutetravels.in.** 2016 Bootstrap-3/W3layouts template, Lorem Ipsum, copied competitor text, low-contrast text, no real visuals — the explicit baseline this project exists to replace (`company_info.md` §5).

## Design Principles

1. **Evolve, don't replace.** Gold/champagne + deep navy + Playfair Display stays the identity. "Modern" is achieved through layout, motion, imagery, and color *conviction* — not a palette swap.
2. **Commit harder to the existing palette.** Move the color strategy from restrained (quiet accent, mostly neutral) toward more confident use of gold at conversion moments and navy as a bigger structural device — without introducing new hue families.
3. **Trust through specificity, not decoration.** Real numbers (years operating, clients served, destinations), real process, real credibility signals — emerald is promoted to do this job visually (verified/trusted signal color), rather than being a quiet, underused secondary.
4. **Two audiences, one visit.** Corporate/MICE credibility and leisure/FIT warmth both need to land without either audience feeling like an afterthought.
5. **Request-a-quote, not search-and-book.** Don't borrow OTA interaction patterns (search bars, filter grids, listing cards) that imply live inventory this business doesn't have.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA (already a stated constraint in `DESIGN.md` — gold's text-contrast variant exists specifically for this). No additional requirements raised; assume standard AA as the bar for any new component.
