How It Works

Four Steps, Zero Surprises

The same process whether it's one leisure applicant or a 40-person corporate delegation.

Step 01

Eligibility Review

We look at your travel history, financials and purpose of visit before you pay a single embassy fee — and tell you honestly if the timing is wrong.

Step 02

Document Pre-Audit

Every statement, ITR and cover letter is checked line by line against the current embassy checklist. This is where most rejections are prevented.

Step 03

Appointment & Filing

We book the VFS or embassy slot, assemble the file in the required order, and brief you on exactly what to expect at the counter.

Step 04

Tracking To Decision

You get status updates from us, not a portal. If the embassy raises a query, we handle the response the same day.
